How it works

  1. Pick 5, 10 or 30 seconds.
  2. Your first click starts the timer. Keep clicking until it reaches zero.
  3. Your CPS is the number of clicks divided by the seconds. Your best score is saved in this browser.

What is a good CPS?

Most people land between 4 and 7 CPS. Above 8 is fast, and anything over 10 is exceptional. Longer tests are harder, because your hand gets tired.

Questions

What does CPS mean?

CPS stands for clicks per second, a simple way to measure how fast you can tap or click.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Tap the big button with one or more fingers. Scores can differ between a phone and a mouse.

Do you store my results?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy.
