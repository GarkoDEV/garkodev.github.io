    How it works

    1. Pick a gold or silver coin.
    2. Tap the coin or the button to flip it.
    3. The result is chosen at random with a 50% chance for heads and 50% for tails, and your stats are saved in this browser.

    Questions

    Is the coin flip fair?

    Yes. Each flip uses your browser's random number generator, so heads and tails are equally likely and every flip is independent of the previous one.

    Why do I sometimes get the same result many times in a row?

    Streaks are normal in random results. Over a small number of flips you can easily see 4 or 5 of the same side. The balance only evens out over many flips.

    Do you store my results?

    Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy.
