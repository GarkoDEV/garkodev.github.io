How to play

  1. Tap the screen, click the game or press the space bar to flap.
  2. Fly through the gap between each pair of pipes to score a point.
  3. Touching a pipe or the ground ends the run. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Tips

Use short, regular taps instead of hammering the screen, and keep the bird near the middle of the gap. Do not look at the bird: watch the next pipe.

Questions

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game adapts to the screen and you control it with a tap anywhere on the game area.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs directly in your browser. If you like tapping games, you can also find my free Android games on Google Play.

Do you store my score?

Only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy.
