About this game

Take control of a powerful police car and chase criminals down endless highways. Weave through traffic, intercept lawbreakers and complete missions where every second counts.

Choose your difficulty: Easy for a relaxed chase, Normal for tougher enemies and more intense pursuits, or Hard if you want a real test. You can customise your car's licence plate with your own call sign or name, and spend coins on tools such as emergency brakes or turbo boosts.

The game is free to play. Ads are optional and give you perks such as a shield that saves you from one crash during a mission, or double rewards after a successful pursuit. Sirens, crashes and a background soundtrack keep the chase exciting.

Features

  • Highway pursuit missions with traffic to dodge
  • Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty modes
  • Customisable licence plate
  • Coins to buy emergency brakes and turbo boosts
  • Optional ads for a shield or double rewards
  • Sirens, crash effects and a soundtrack
