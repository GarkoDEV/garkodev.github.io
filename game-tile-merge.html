Tile Merge: a 2048-style puzzle

Slide the tiles and merge two equal numbers into one. Keep merging to reach 2048, and keep going if you want a higher score. It is free, runs in your browser and works with swipes on your phone or arrow keys on your computer.

How to play

  1. Swipe on the board or use the arrow keys (or W, A, S, D) to slide all tiles in that direction.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value and add to your score.
  3. After every move a new 2 or 4 appears. The game ends when the board is full and no merges are left.

Tips

Keep your biggest tile in a corner and build a chain of decreasing numbers next to it. Pick two or three directions to use most of the time and avoid swiping against your corner unless you have no other move.

Questions

What is the goal?

Create a tile with the number 2048. You can keep playing afterwards to reach an even higher score.

Can I undo a move?

Yes, the Undo button takes back your last move. Only the latest move can be undone.

Do you store my score?

Only your best score, on your own device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy.
