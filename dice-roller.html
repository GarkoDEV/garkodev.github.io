    How it works

    1. Choose how many dice you want (1 to 6) and how many sides they have.
    2. Tap the button or the dice to roll them.
    3. Each die shows its own result and the total is added up for you. Your last rolls stay listed below.

    Questions

    Are the rolls fair?

    Yes. Each die uses your browser's random number generator, so every face is equally likely and every die is independent of the others.

    Can I use it for role-playing games?

    Yes. Pick d20 for attack and skill checks, d4 to d12 for damage, and up to six dice at once for bigger rolls like fireballs.

    Do you store my rolls?

    No. Rolls are only shown on your screen while you use the page and are not saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
